The Beatles

The Beatles were a British rock band formed in Liverpool in 1960. The group included John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, and Ringo Starr. They became famous for their catchy songs, distinctive style, and huge influence on popular music.

Two of their most famous albums are Abbey Road and Sgt. Pepper’s Lonely Hearts Club Band. Songs such as “Hey Jude” and “Let It Be” show their talent for memorable melodies and meaningful lyrics. Their music continues to inspire musicians and fans around the world.
