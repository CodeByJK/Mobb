Pink Floyd

Pink Floyd were a British rock band formed in London in 1965. They became famous for their creative sound, thoughtful lyrics, and spectacular live shows. Their music combined rock with experimental sounds and often explored themes such as time, isolation, and society.

Two of their most famous albums are "The Dark Side of the Moon" and "The Wall". Songs such as “Wish You Were Here” and “Comfortably Numb” showcase their emotional lyrics and memorable guitar playing. Their music has influenced generations of musicians and listeners.
