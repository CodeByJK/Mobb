The Rolling Stones

The Rolling Stones are a British rock band formed in London in 1962. They became famous for their energetic performances and a sound rooted in blues and rock and roll. Singer Mick Jagger and guitarist Keith Richards are two of the band’s best-known members.

Two of their most famous albums are Sticky Fingers and Exile on Main St. Songs such as “Paint It Black” and “(I Can’t Get No) Satisfaction” showcase their memorable guitar riffs and distinctive style. Their music has influenced generations of rock musicians and fans.
